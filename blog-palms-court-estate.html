Inside Palms Court Estate: our latest build in Ajah, Lagos

September 2026 · JPV Brand

Palms Court Estate is our newest residential development in Ajah, on the Lagos mainland's growing eastern corridor. It's a 14-unit estate of 4‑bedroom detached houses, and it's currently under construction, with a target completion in Q4 2026.

We get a lot of questions about what's actually going into a project like this, so here's a straightforward look at where things stand.

What's included

Each of the 14 units is a 4‑bedroom detached house. Beyond the homes themselves, the estate is being built with shared amenities that go a step further than a typical single-family plot:

  • A swimming pool for residents
  • An on-site tennis court
  • A dedicated kids' play area

The idea behind Palms Court, like our other estates, is to build a self-contained residential community rather than just a row of houses, somewhere families can actually use the shared spaces day to day.

Why Ajah

Ajah has been one of the fastest-growing residential areas on the Lagos mainland over the past decade, as the city's centre of gravity keeps shifting east along the Lekki-Epe corridor. It's close enough to established commercial areas to be practical for daily commuting, while still offering more room to build the kind of low-density, amenity-led estate that a dense, older part of the city wouldn't have space for.

What "Q4 2026" actually means

We're upfront on our Africa projects page that Palms Court is still under construction, and we keep that status updated rather than leaving an old date sitting there indefinitely. Construction timelines on a multi-unit estate can shift for reasons outside anyone's control, weather, material lead times, permitting, so we'd rather tell you honestly where things stand than promise a date we can't guarantee. If you're seriously considering a unit, the most accurate answer is always the one we can give you directly, today, not what's printed on a page.
