Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

JPV Brand ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It covers jpvbrand.com and the JPLUX UK product pages hosted on it.

Information we collect

We only receive information that you choose to give us. This happens when you:

  • Fill in the contact form, feedback form, or newsletter sign-up
  • Use the bulk order request feature on our Products or JPLUX UK pages
  • Email, call, or message us directly

Depending on which of these you use, this can include your name, company name, email address, phone number, delivery region, and the products or quantities you're asking about. We don't collect payment details anywhere on this site, since we don't process payments here.

How we use your information

We use the information you share with us to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes and pricing, and follow up on bulk orders or project questions. We don't use it for anything beyond what you contacted us about, and we don't send marketing emails unless you've specifically signed up for updates.

How your information reaches us

Our contact, feedback, and order forms open your own email app with a pre-filled message addressed to us; nothing is submitted until you personally choose to send that email. Because of this, we only ever see what you actually send.

Local browser storage

To make browsing more convenient, some features (like the bulk order request list and its contact fields) save what you've entered in your own browser, so it's still there if you come back to the page later or move between our Products and JPLUX UK pages. This information stays on your device and belongs to you: we cannot see it, and it is never transmitted anywhere unless you choose to send it to us through the order request or contact form. You can clear it at any time using your browser's site data settings, or the "Clear list" option in the order panel.

Cookies and third-party services

This site itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies. A few embedded third-party services may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts, used to load the typefaces on this site
  • A Google Maps embed on our Contact page, showing our Dubai office location
  • Links to WhatsApp, X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok, which may track visits to their own platforms once you click through

We don't control these third-party services and recommend checking their own privacy policies if you have questions about how they handle data.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone. It's used internally by JPV Brand to respond to your enquiry, and may be shared with our own team members or logistics partners only where necessary to fulfil an order or shipment you've requested.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, fulfil an order, and maintain standard business records, and then delete or archive it in the ordinary course of business.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do this, email us at contact@jpvbrand.com.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or business practices change. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, reach out to contact@jpvbrand.com or +971 4 572 3616.
