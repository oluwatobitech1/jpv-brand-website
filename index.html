The JPV story

In 2000, our founders, Chairman and Co‑CEOs established JPV Brand, the parent company of the JPV Brand Group, with a bold vision: to lead in both real estate development and international trade, guided by integrity, excellence, and long-term value.

From the beginning, JPV Brand recognised two essential needs shaping Africa and beyond:

  • The need for quality homes and sustainable real estate projects.
  • The need for reliable access to heavy machinery, spare parts, and building materials to fuel growth and development.

Driven by a winning strategy and a people-first approach, we continue to meet these needs, guided by our mission of enriching lives and empowering communities by doing things the right way.

Over the years, we have learned what matters most: trust, quality, and reliability. That's why we are committed to:

  • Delivering beautiful living environments and innovative real estate projects that enhance the lifestyle of our residents.
  • Providing dependable export services from the UAE and China to West Africa, ensuring businesses, contractors, and developers receive the tools and resources they need to succeed.

At JPV, we take pride in knowing that we are more than a business — we are a partner in building futures. Whether it's creating homes where families celebrate life's milestones, or supplying the materials and machinery that power infrastructure, JPV Brand stands for confidence, quality, and growth.

Frequently asked questions

A few things people usually ask us

What does JPV Brand actually do?

JPV Brand has a dual focus: property development (through JP Estate and JP Views & Suites) and international trade — exporting heavy construction machinery, spare parts, and building materials from the UAE and China to Africa and Europe through JPV Auto Spare Parts Trading LLC.

Where does JPV Brand operate?

Our head office is in Dubai, UAE, with active property developments across Lagos, Nigeria, commercial projects across the Middle East, an export network into China, and our Europe operations launching soon.
