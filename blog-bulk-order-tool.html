How our bulk order request tool actually works

September 2026 · JPV Brand

If you've browsed our Products or JPLUX UK pages, you'll have noticed something a typical online store doesn't have: no prices, and no checkout. That's intentional, most of what we supply moves in bulk, and pricing depends on quantity and where it's shipping to, so a fixed price tag wouldn't be accurate anyway. Here's what the "Bulk Order Request" panel actually does instead.

Building your list

Every product has a quantity stepper and an "Add to list" button. As you add items, they collect in a running list you can open at any time from the panel icon in the header, the same list follows you as you move between the Products and JPLUX UK pages, so you don't have to submit everything from one page at once.

What happens when you send it

Before you send your request, we ask for a few basics: your company name, a contact person, and an email address, plus optional fields for phone, delivery region, and any notes. When you hit "Send bulk order request," this opens your own email app with a message already written out, addressed to us, listing everything in your request grouped by category. Nothing is submitted automatically in the background; you're sending a real email from your own account, the same as if you'd typed it yourself.

Why we built it this way

We don't have a live payment system on this site, and we didn't want to fake one. A lot of sites bolt on a shopping cart because that's the familiar pattern, even when there's no real checkout behind it. We'd rather be upfront: this tool exists to save you from re-typing a long list of products and quantities into an email by hand, not to pretend we're processing an order in real time. Once we receive your request, a real person on our team follows up with actual pricing, availability, and shipping options for your quantities and region.

Your list is saved locally

If you leave the page and come back, your list and contact details are remembered in your own browser, not on our servers, so you can keep adding to it over multiple visits before you're ready to send.
